# Quality bar

Publish a piece only when it passes the checklist and scores at least Solid on every rubric row. A Weak score in any row sends it back to Draft.

### Pre-publish checklist

**Content**

- The title states the outcome.
- The summary, five sentences or fewer, gives problem, approach, a result with a number, and one lesson.
- Your role and the team's role are explicit.
- Every success claim has evidence beside it.
- At least one decision shows the alternatives you weighed.
- Dead ends appear, with what each taught.
- Dates, versions and environment are stated.

**Reproducibility**

- Someone else, or you on a clean machine or snapshot, followed the steps and they worked.
- Commands are copyable text; placeholders are marked and explained.

**Safety**

- The redaction pass is done, and any exposed secret has been rotated.
- Metadata is stripped from images and documents.
- Permission is on file for work-derived material, and platform rules are checked.

**Presentation**

- Headings state findings; paragraphs run three sentences or fewer.
- Every image has a caption and alt text.
- A link checker passes.
- Card, writeup and repo all link to each other.

### Scoring rubric

| Criterion | Weak (1) | Solid (2) | Exemplary (3) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Purpose | Reader can't tell why it exists | Goal and reader clear from the summary | The summary alone would earn a callback |
| Evidence | Claims without proof | Proof beside every key claim | Proof the reader can download or rerun |
| Reasoning | Steps only | Key decisions explained | Alternatives and trade-offs compared |
| Reproducibility | Versions or steps missing | A peer could repeat it with effort | A clean rerun has been verified |
| Results | No outcome stated | Outcome stated | Before and after quantified, with sources |
| Reflection | None | Lessons listed | Dead ends explained; lessons changed the next project |
| Presentation | Wall of text | Skimmable structure | All four depth layers exist and link |
| Safety | Not checked | Redaction pass done | A second reviewer confirmed it |
