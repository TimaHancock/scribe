# Decision record

Write one per real choice, in `decisions/NNN-slug.md`, numbered in order and never renumbered. The structure follows Michael Nygard's ADR format, with an options section added from design-doc practice.

```markdown
# NNN. <Decision as a short noun phrase>
Date: YYYY-MM-DD · Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNN

## Context
The facts and forces in tension. No opinions yet.

## Options considered
1. Option A: what it gives, what it costs
2. Option B: what it gives, what it costs

## Decision
We will ... (a full sentence, active voice)

## Consequences
Positive, negative and neutral effects, including what this makes harder later.
```
