# Master writeup

The full skeleton in reading order. Cut sections only where the [Anatomy table](../playbook/04-anatomy-of-a-strong-writeup.md) allows it, and write the Summary last.

```markdown
# <Outcome-oriented title>

| Date | Status | Time spent | My role | Stack | Skills shown | Links |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YYYY-MM-DD | Published; last verified YYYY-MM-DD | ~N hours | Solo, lead or contributor | ... | ... | Repo · Demo |

## Summary
Three to five sentences: the problem, what you did, the result with a number, and one lesson.

## Context and problem
Why this mattered, and to whom.

## Goals and non-goals
- Goal: ...
- Non-goal: ... (and why it was out)

## Environment
Diagram, components and versions: everything a reader needs to picture the setup.

## Approach and decisions
The plan, the options you weighed, and why you chose this path. Link decision records.

## Walkthrough
### 1. <Step, stated as its outcome>
**Why:** ...
**What:** the command or action, as copyable text
**Proof:** output, or a screenshot with a caption and alt text

## Results
Before and after, with units and the evidence behind each number.

## Challenges and dead ends
What broke, how you diagnosed it, what it cost, and what it taught.

## Going further (optional)
What you explored after the goal was met, and why it works.

## Lessons and next steps
- What went well
- What went wrong
- Where you got lucky
- What you would do next, or with twice the time

## References and credits
Sources, tools, collaborators, and any AI assistance: what it did and what you verified.

## Appendix
Full configs, scripts, raw output, indicators, glossary.

## Changelog
- YYYY-MM-DD: First published.
```
