# Short forms

Distill these from the finished writeup, never the other way round. Each one links down to the full piece.

```markdown
### Portfolio card
**<Title>** · YYYY-MM
<One-line outcome with a number.>
Tags: <skill>, <skill>, <tool>
[Writeup](url) · [Repo](url) · [Demo](url)

### Resume bullet
<Action verb> <what> using <how>, <result with a number> (writeup link).

### Social post
<The result or the surprising lesson, in one sentence.>
<What you built or learned, in two sentences.>
<One screenshot or GIF.>
<Link to the writeup.>
```
