# Retrospective and brag-doc entry

Run the retro within two days of finishing, during Distill. The brag-doc entry goes into your running brag document, and gets a follow-up line months later when real results are in.

```markdown
## Retro
- The result, in one sentence with a number:
- What went well:
- What went wrong:
- Where I got lucky:
- The decision I would make differently:
- The dead end that cost most, and what would have caught it sooner:
- What I will reuse (script, template, technique):
- What I would do with twice the time:

## Brag-doc entry
**<Project>** · YYYY-MM
- Goal: why it mattered
- My contribution: what I designed, built or decided
- Impact: who benefited, with numbers where possible
- Skills: what I learned or demonstrated
- Follow-up (YYYY-MM-DD): what actually happened afterward
```
